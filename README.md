### 1. Empirical Economic Research Paper — Tariffs as a Tax on the Poor
* **File:** `TARIFFS AS A TAX ON THE POOR.docx`
* **Architecture:** Difference-in-Differences (DiD) econometric framework utilizing BLS Consumer Price Index microdata (January–December 2025) matched to 2024 Consumer Expenditure Survey (CEX) quintile expenditure baskets.
* **Key Mechanics:** Exploits the U.S. Supreme Court's May 28, 2025 invalidation of International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) tariff authority as a natural policy experiment, reducing the average effective tariff from 18.3% to 9.1%. Calibrated against the Yale Budget Lab Tariff Incidence Model.
* **Key Findings:** Establishes that the 2025 IEEPA tariff regime functioned as a regressive implicit consumption tax. Bottom-quintile households sustained welfare losses equal to **7.6% of pretax income ($1,300)** versus **1.3% for the top quintile ($4,550)**—a **5.8-fold distributional gradient**. DiD estimates confirm a statistically significant price effect ($\beta_3 = -0.029, p < 0.01$) concentrated in tariff-exposed necessity goods (apparel, footwear, food), with bottom-quintile loss reductions reaching **69%** post-SCOTUS ruling.

### 2. LBO Model & Sponsor Returns — Jubilant Foodworks Ltd.
* **File:** `LBO LBO.pdf`
* **Architecture:** 5-year leveraged buyout (LBO) transaction model with detailed operating assumptions, debt schedule, cash sweep, and exit return calculations.
* **Key Mechanics:** Models an entry EV/EBITDA multiple of **17.6x** ($3,484.8M Enterprise Value) funded with **4.0x Total Debt/EBITDA** ($792M debt) and an initial sponsor equity check of **$2,692.8M** (FX Rate USD/INR at 95.175). Incorporates revenue growth stepping down from 15% to 10%, EBITDA margin expansion from 19.80% to 21.80%, an 8.90% interest rate, and an 80% cash sweep.
* **Outputs:** Projects an exit Enterprise Value of **$6,087.81M** in Year 5, generating an Exit Equity Value of **$5,474M**, a **2.03x Multiple on Invested Capital (MOIC)**, and a **15% IRR**.

### 3. M&A Merger Model — UBS Group AG / Commerzbank AG
* **File:** `UBS CBK.xlsx`
* **Architecture:** Comprehensive 7-sheet institutional M&A transaction model (`ASSUMPTIONS`, `UBS STANDALONE`, `CBK STANDALONE`, `CCA COMPS`, `M&A ENGINE`).
* **Key Mechanics:** Models a 100%-stock acquisition of Commerzbank AG by UBS Group AG at a 24% premium (exchange ratio of 1.105x based on Dec 31, 2025 share prices), incorporating Purchase Price Allocation (PPA), intangible asset write-ups, deferred tax liabilities, and phased cost/revenue synergies (60%/85%/100%).
* **Outputs:** Evaluates accretion/dilution across a European banking comparable set (Deutsche Bank, etc.), defending a realistic non-accretive read (-20.89% in Year 1 to -17.03% in Year 3) driven by share issuance dilution exceeding immediate run-rate synergies.

### 4. Equity Research, CCM & DCF Model — Infosys Limited (INFY)
* **Files:** `Infosys 33.xlsx` & `DCF.xlsx`
* **Architecture:** Integrated institutional valuation and comparable company modeling suite tracking FY 2021 to FY 2025 actuals alongside 5-year projections.
* **Key Mechanics:** Unlevered and relevered peer beta benchmarking against an Indian IT services comp set (TCS, HCL, Wipro, Tech Mahindra), 13% WACC derivation, 6% terminal growth rate, explicit free cash flow forecasting, and sensitivity matrices.
* **Outputs:** Revenue compound annual growth rate (**12.86% CAGR** from FY21–FY25, scaling from ₹1,00,472 Cr to ₹1,62,990 Cr), underpinning an intrinsic equity valuation range and fundamental investment thesis.

### 5. Horizontal Financial Statement Analysis — Apple Inc. (AAPL)
* **File:** `apple_horizontal.xlsx`

* **Key Mechanics:** Tracks revenue scaling from **$383,285M (FY23)** to **$416,161M (FY25)**, mapping cost of sales, R&D ($34,550M in FY25), SG&A, and operating leverage.
* **Outputs:** Derives robust gross margins (**$195,201M** or **46.9%** in FY25), operating income ($133,050M EBIT), and EBITDA ($144,748M), establishing baseline cash-generation mechanics for tech sector valuation.
